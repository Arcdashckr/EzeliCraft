## 🎮 MINECRAFT EZELICRAFT: ASCEND

### 🛡️ Equipment (Ekipman)

🔹 **[Jake's Build Tools](https://modrinth.com/mod/jakes-build-tools)**
> 📝 Adds a bunch of craftable tools that help make building easier! Trowel, chisel, hammer and so much more!

🔹 **[Craftable Trident](https://modrinth.com/mod/craft-tridents)**
> 📝 Craft tridents with breeze rods and prismarine shards!

🔹 **[Netherite Compass](https://modrinth.com/mod/netherite-compass)**
> 📝 This mod adds the Netherite Compass to your game which helps you track down Ancient Debris blocks in you world.

🔹 **[Improved Trident](https://modrinth.com/mod/improved-trident)**
> 📝 Now you can use Riptide with Channeling and Loyalty without problem

🔹 **[Infinity Mending](https://modrinth.com/mod/infinitymendingbows)**
> 📝 Makes the mending and infinity enchantments compatible with each other!

🔹 **[Cheaper Armor Trim Duplication](https://modrinth.com/mod/cheaper-armor-trim-duplication)**
> 📝 Makes armor trim duplication cheaper.

🔹 **[Craft TNT from Gravel](https://modrinth.com/mod/craft-tnt-from-gravel)**
> 📝 This data pack allows you to craft TNT from gravel and gunpowder. 

The sand recipe is also kept so it allows you more choice when crafting TNT.

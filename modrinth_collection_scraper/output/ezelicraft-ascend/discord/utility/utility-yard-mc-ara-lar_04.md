## 🎮 MINECRAFT EZELICRAFT: ASCEND

### 🛠️ Utility (Yardımcı Araçlar)

🔹 **[Recursive Resources](https://modrinth.com/mod/recursiveresources)**
> 📝 Enhances the Resource Pack menu with folder support.

🔹 **[Chat Signing Hider](https://modrinth.com/mod/chat-signing-hider)**
> 📝 A client side Fabric mod that hides all things related to chat signing

🔹 **[Damage Numbers](https://modrinth.com/mod/damagenumbers)**
> 📝 Adds simple damage number particles when any entity takes damage

🔹 **[Servux](https://modrinth.com/mod/servux)**
> 📝 Servux is a server-side mod that provides support for some client-side mods, such as sending structure bounding boxes for MiniHUD

🔹 **[Status](https://modrinth.com/mod/status)**
> 📝 The status mod used on the Hermitcraft server.

🔹 **[Portable Crafting](https://modrinth.com/mod/portable-crafting)**
> 📝 Use crafting stations (tables, smithing, anvils, stonecutters, etc) directly from your inventory.

🔹 **[MinecraftCapes](https://modrinth.com/mod/minecraftcapes)**
> 📝 Custom Capes anyone with the mod can see

🔹 **[Chat Notify](https://modrinth.com/mod/chatnotify)**
> 📝 Plays a ping sound when your name is mentioned, with options to create custom alerts.

🔹 **[Simple Voice Chat Enhanced Groups](https://modrinth.com/mod/enhanced-groups)**
> 📝 A server side Fabric mod providing useful features to Simple Voice Chat groups.

🔹 **[Isometric Renders](https://modrinth.com/mod/isometric-renders)**
> 📝 Creates high-resolution isometric screenshots of game objects

🔹 **[Styled Chat](https://modrinth.com/mod/styled-chat)**
> 📝 Style your server's chat as you want!

🔹 **[Improved Signs](https://modrinth.com/mod/improved-signs)**
> 📝 Improvements for your signs and frames on your Fabric server. Passthrough signs, retain sign text, copy signs & more!

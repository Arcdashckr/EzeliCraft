> 📝 🎨 Ability to restore/change skins on servers! (Offline and Online Mode)

🔹 **[AudioPlayer](https://modrinth.com/mod/audioplayer)**
> 📝 Put your own audio files on music discs!

🔹 **[MasterCutter](https://modrinth.com/mod/mastercutter)**
> 📝 The stonecutter is now more useful than ever with over 500 new recipes including woodcutting, enhanced base stonecutting, recycling and "un-crafting".

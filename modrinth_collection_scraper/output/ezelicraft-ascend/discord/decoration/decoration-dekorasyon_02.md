## 🎮 MINECRAFT EZELICRAFT: ASCEND

### 🧱 Decoration (Dekorasyon)

🔹 **[Animatica](https://modrinth.com/mod/animatica)**
> 📝 A mod implementing the OptiFine/MCPatcher animated texture format

🔹 **[Chipped](https://modrinth.com/mod/chipped)**
> 📝 Every block deserves a friend.

🔹 **[Particle Rain](https://modrinth.com/mod/particle-rain)**
> 📝 Replaces weather with prettier particle effects

🔹 **[YUNG's Bridges](https://modrinth.com/mod/yungs-bridges)**
> 📝 Adds beautiful naturally generated bridges throughout the world!

🔹 **[Farmer's Delight Refabricated](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight-refabricated)**
> 📝 Modern Fabric port of the cooking and farming mod, "Farmer's Delight"

🔹 **[YUNG's Extras](https://modrinth.com/mod/yungs-extras)**
> 📝 Extra structures, features, and vanilla+ content suitable for any modpack!

🔹 **[Macaw's Fences and Walls](https://modrinth.com/mod/macaws-fences-and-walls)**
> 📝 Adds new vanilla styled fences, walls and gates!

🔹 **[Macaw's Windows](https://modrinth.com/mod/macaws-windows)**
> 📝 Adds lots of Windows, Mosaic Glass, Blinds, Shutters, Curtains and more...!

🔹 **[Highlight](https://modrinth.com/mod/highlight)**
> 📝 Highlight changes the visualization of specific blocks in the game to have more aligned selections to their models.

🔹 **[Better Beds](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-beds)**
> 📝 Changes the renderer of the bed to use json models instead of a block entity renderer!

🔹 **[Macaw's Bridges](https://modrinth.com/mod/macaws-bridges)**
> 📝 A simple mod that adds a lot of bridges!

🔹 **[Chef's Delight - Farmer's Delight Villagers](https://modrinth.com/mod/chefs-delight)**
> 📝 Add-on for the Farmer's Delight mod. Adds 2 new professions to villagers. Chef and Cook.

Depends on Farmer's Delight.

## 🎮 MINECRAFT EZELICRAFT: ASCEND

### ⚙️ Game Mechanics (Oyun Mekanikleri)

🔹 **[Conduits Prevent Drowned](https://modrinth.com/mod/conduits-prevent-drowned)**
> 📝 ❌ Disables drowned zombie spawns around the player when the conduit effect is active.

🔹 **[Deepslate Tweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/deepslate-tweaks)**
> 📝 This mod changes the speed at which you mine Deepslate to that of normal stone. No longer will you be dreading mining in the depths of your world.

🔹 **[Sticky Enchanting Lapis](https://modrinth.com/mod/sticky-enchanting-lapis)**
> 📝 📖🔷 Allows lapis lazuli to remain in the enchanting table and be shared among players.

🔹 **[Milk All The Mobs](https://modrinth.com/mod/milk-all-the-mobs)**
> 📝 🥛 Allows sheep, llamas, pigs, donkeys, horses and mules to produce a bucket of milk.

🔹 **[Integrated circuit](https://modrinth.com/mod/integrated-circuit)**
> 📝 Adds integrated circuits to compact your redstone circuits into one block

🔹 **[Third Person Death](https://modrinth.com/mod/thirdpersondeath)**
> 📝 Bedrock-styled cinematic death camera for Java Edition.

🔹 **[Nemo's Mending](https://modrinth.com/mod/nemos-mending)**
> 📝 Repair your mending tools even if they are not in your equipment slots!

🔹 **[Jump-proof Farmland](https://modrinth.com/mod/jumpprooffarmland)**
> 📝 Prevents farmland to convert back to dirt when jumping on it

🔹 **[Useful Snow Golems](https://modrinth.com/mod/useful-snow-golems)**
> 📝 Snow Golems do real damage to hostile mobs!

🔹 **[OmniBlast: Blast Furnace and Smoker recipe list completed](https://modrinth.com/mod/omniblast)**
> 📝 OmniBlast is a quality of life Datapack for Minecraft that completes the recipe lists of the Blast Furnace and Smoker to enable faster cooking for blocks and items that have only a standard Furnace recipe (e.g., Glass, Glazed Terracota, Cactus, etc.)

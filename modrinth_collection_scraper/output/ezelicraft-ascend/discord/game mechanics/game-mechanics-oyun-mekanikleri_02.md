## 🎮 MINECRAFT EZELICRAFT: ASCEND

### ⚙️ Game Mechanics (Oyun Mekanikleri)

🔹 **[Inventory Totem](https://modrinth.com/mod/inventory-totem)**
> 📝 🎴 Makes the totem of undying effect work when it's anywhere in the inventory.

🔹 **[Infinite Trading](https://modrinth.com/mod/infinite-trading)**
> 📝 ♾ Prevents villager trades from locking up, making them always available. Trade away!

🔹 **[Scaffolding Drops Nearby](https://modrinth.com/mod/scaffolding-drops-nearby)**
> 📝 🏗 When breaking a scaffolding block, all chained blocks will drop at the block's position.

🔹 **[Leaf Me Alone](https://modrinth.com/mod/leaf-me-alone)**
> 📝 Allows any rideable entity with controlling it passenger to move trough leaves

🔹 **[No Animal Tempt Delay](https://modrinth.com/mod/no-animal-tempt-delay)**
> 📝 😍 Removes the cooldown in between trying to attract animals with their desired food.

🔹 **[More Mobs](https://modrinth.com/mod/more-mobs)**
> 📝 Humanoid mobs get new variants through over 85 custom player heads or unused vanilla mobs! No Resource Pack needed!

🔹 **[Armor Poser](https://modrinth.com/mod/armor-poser)**
> 📝 Adds a GUI for armor stands in which you can configure it's pose and other properties

🔹 **[Enhanced Mob Spawners](https://modrinth.com/mod/enhanced-mob-spawners)**
> 📝 Goal of this mod is to add more functionality to the original minecraft spawner.

🔹 **[BlazeandCave's Advancements Pack](https://modrinth.com/mod/blazeandcaves-advancements-pack)**
> 📝 Adds over 900 new advancements to your Minecraft world

🔹 **[Extended Bone Meal](https://modrinth.com/mod/extended-bone-meal)**
> 📝 ☘ Extends bonemeal behaviour. Instant grow with sneak, grow netherwart+cacti+sugarcane and other tweaks.

🔹 **[Click Villagers](https://modrinth.com/mod/clickvillagers)**
> 📝 Click to pick up villagers, reset their trades, change their biomes, claim them and much more!
